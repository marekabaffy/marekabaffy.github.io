Privacy Policy

Marek's Bot is a private, personal assistant operated by Marek Abaffy for his own use.

The app accesses only its own dedicated Google account's Calendar and Drive, to manage the events and files its owner asks for. Content is processed by the owner's AI model provider to answer requests and is not sold or shared with anyone else. No third parties' data is collected.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
